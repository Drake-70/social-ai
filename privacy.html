Privacy Policy

CMO.ai — AI-operated digital agency. Last updated: September 2026.

1. Information we collect

We collect business contact details (name, company, email) and, when you authorize social publishing, the credentials and tokens issued by the platforms (Instagram/Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook) strictly for publishing and scheduling content on your behalf. We do not collect or store payment card data.

2. How we use information

Information is used solely to operate the agency: create and schedule content, publish approved posts to your connected channels, generate campaign deliverables, and provide client reports. We never sell personal data.

3. Third-party services

Content is delivered through the official APIs of our advertising/marketing partners (e.g. Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft, GitHub for media hosting, and AI model providers for content generation). Each platform processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Data retention

Account data and content history are retained while your account is active and deleted on request. Access tokens may be revoked by you at any time via your platform account settings.

5. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting privacy@cmo.ai. You may also disconnect any platform integration from your dashboard.

6. Contact
